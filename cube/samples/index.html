Sample cubes

Each link opens the coach (../solve.html) with the colours already painted, so a scenario can be tried without colouring six faces again. Press Solve it on the page that loads to see what the coach says about them.

Rebuild these files with node cube/samples/generate.js.

missing

  • A few stickers left unpainted
    Four stickers have no colour. The page should point at them and ask for the rest.
  • A whole face left unpainted
    The back face is empty, as if the child stopped before turning the cube round.

centres

  • Two centres painted the same colour
    The back centre repeats the top centre, so one colour is missing from the middles.
  • Three centres painted the same colour
    Two middles were overpainted, so two colours are missing and no single repaint fixes it.

count

  • One sticker the wrong colour
    Ten of one colour, eight of another: the classic mis-read. One repaint should fix it.
  • Two stickers the wrong colour
    Two stickers read as the same wrong colour, so the page has pairs of repaints to offer.
  • Four stickers the wrong colour
    Too far off to guess a repair: the page should show the counts and leave the fixing to the child.

twist

  • A corner twisted in place
    The three colours of one corner were entered rotated by one.
  • A corner twisted the other way
    Another corner, rotated the opposite way, on a different part of the cube.

flip

  • An edge flipped in place
    The two colours of one edge were entered the wrong way round.
  • An edge flipped, further round the cube
    The same mistake on an edge that is harder to spot.

parity

  • Two edges swapped
    Two whole edge pieces have traded places, which a real cube cannot do.
  • Two corners swapped
    The same impossible trade, with corners instead of edges.

pieces

  • A corner with colours that cannot meet
    Two stickers traded places across pieces, leaving a corner no real cube has.
  • An edge with colours that cannot meet
    The same kind of trade, this time producing an impossible edge.
  • Two corners that look like the same piece
    Both corners now carry the same three colours.
  • Two edges that look like the same piece
    Both edges now carry the same two colours.

corner-order

  • A corner with its colours in mirrored order
    The right three colours on a corner, but read round the wrong way.

valid cube

  • A valid cube (control)
    The plain scramble every other sample is built from. It should solve, not complain.
